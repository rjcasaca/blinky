# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome; review and maintenance are best effort. Check [README.md](README.md), [WINDOWS-VALIDATION.md](WINDOWS-VALIDATION.md) and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) first.

By intentionally submitting a contribution for inclusion, you agree to license your contribution under the project's MIT License. You retain your copyright. Submit only material you have the right to contribute; preserve third-party notices and disclose added dependencies and licenses. No copyright assignment or separate contributor agreement is required.

Use synthetic or explicitly authorized samples. Do not commit recordings, transcripts, student/participant information, credentials, models, generated client configuration or local settings. Keep machine-specific paths and build artifacts out of source control. Report security-sensitive issues privately.

Describe the change, its effect, validation performed and remaining limitations. Run checks appropriate to the change. Claims about live Windows recording or meeting behavior should identify the actual Windows/device/application environment tested; a Linux cross-build is not that verification.
